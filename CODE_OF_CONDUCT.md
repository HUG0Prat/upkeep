# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want UpKeep to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, friendly, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a language that is not their own.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences; disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the users and the community, not only for ourselves.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention or advances.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information (such as an address or e-mail) without their explicit permission.
- Spamming, or repeatedly pinging people to get attention.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, commit messages — and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the maintainers:

- use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue concerned, and/or
- open a private report through the repository's [security advisory form](https://github.com/HUG0Prat/upkeep/security/advisories/new) with the title **"Code of Conduct report"** if the matter needs confidentiality.

All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of the reporter.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, proportionate to the situation:

1. **Correction** — a private, written explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate, possibly with a request for a public apology.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behavior, and a period without interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban** — no interaction with the project for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban** — for a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face consequences determined by other members of the project's leadership.
