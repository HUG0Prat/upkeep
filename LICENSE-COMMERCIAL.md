# Commercial licensing

UpKeep is **dual-licensed**:

| License | Who it is for | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](LICENSE) | individuals, hobbyists, students, schools, researchers, charities, public bodies — any **noncommercial** use, including modifying and sharing the software | free |
| **UpKeep Commercial License** | any use, modification or redistribution **for a commercial purpose** | paid, on request |

The PolyForm license is the default. If your use is not covered by it, you need a commercial license.

## Do I need a commercial license?

You **do not** need one if you use UpKeep:

- on your own computer for personal use (keeping your PC, games, hobby projects up to date);
- for study, teaching or non-commercial research;
- in a charitable organization, educational institution, public research organization, public safety or health organization, environmental protection organization, or government institution (as defined by the PolyForm Noncommercial license), whatever the funding source;
- to contribute to UpKeep itself.

You **do** need one if, for example:

- a company, freelancer or self-employed professional uses UpKeep on computers used for their business;
- an IT department, managed service provider (MSP) or support company uses UpKeep to maintain computers for itself or for its customers;
- you bundle, pre-install or redistribute UpKeep (modified or not) with a product or service that is sold or that supports a commercial activity;
- you modify UpKeep, or reuse part of its code, in a commercial product or for a commercial purpose;
- you sell services built on UpKeep (paid support, hosting, consulting based on it).

If you are unsure, ask: the answer is free.

## What the commercial license provides

Terms are agreed in writing for each licensee. They typically include:

- the right to use UpKeep for commercial purposes on an agreed number of computers or for an agreed organization;
- if needed, the right to modify UpKeep and to redistribute or embed it in your own products, without having to publish your changes;
- optionally, support and priority handling of issues.

Pricing depends on the size of the organization and the intended use.

## How to request one

Open a discussion in the **Commercial licensing** category of [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/HUG0Prat/upkeep/discussions/categories/commercial-licensing) with:

1. the name of your organization and its country;
2. how you intend to use UpKeep (internal use, number of computers, redistribution, modification);
3. how you would like to be contacted to continue the conversation privately.

Do not post confidential information in the public discussion: it is only used to start the conversation.

## Contributions

To keep dual licensing possible, contributors grant the maintainer the right to distribute their contributions under both licenses. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#licensing-of-contributions).
